Tension analysis

Goldfish

Every hand is dealt from its own seeded shuffle of this exact list, and scored twice — once as if you were on the play, once on the draw. Click any hand to open it and draw through it card by card.
Why the two numbers differ. On the draw you see an eighth card before your first turn, which rescues exactly the hands that are one land short — so a hand you would send back on the play is often worth keeping on the draw. The six-point gap is what that extra card is worth.

Where the defaults come from. They are set so a well-built 60-card deck sends back roughly one hand in eight, which is about what real decks mulligan. They also sit on a flat stretch of the curve: moving the threshold a few points either way barely changes the answer, so you are not balanced on a knife edge. The score itself is a heuristic — open any hand to see the five components behind its number.

Card data from Scryfall, cached locally in your browser. Mana maths follows Frank Karsten's published research, independently re-derived by Monte Carlo simulation — see the Method tab.
